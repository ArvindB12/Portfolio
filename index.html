The Lab.

Behavioral Science isn't magic; it's a rigorous process. Here is how I break down problems, the tools I use, and the mental models that guide my thinking.

My Framework

1. Diagnosis
Identifying the "Behavioral Gap". Why are people acting differently than the optimal model suggests? (e.g., Loss Aversion in crop selection).
2. Hypothesis
Formulating a psychological theory. Is it a lack of ability? A lack of motivation? Or a missing trigger?
3. Intervention Design
Designing the "Nudge" or "Choice Architecture". This is where UX and policy writing happen.
4. Impact Measurement
Using RCTs (Randomized Control Trials) or A/B testing to validate if the behavior actually changed.

Mental Models

The Cobweb Theorem:
Farmers base future production on past prices, causing cycles of volatility. We must break this loop with predictive data.
Hyperbolic Discounting:
Humans prefer smaller, immediate rewards over larger, later ones. Sustainable farming requires bridging this gap.
Narrative Fallacy:
We trust stories more than statistics. To change policy adoption, we must change the story, not just the data.
