KriShiKsha: The Demand-Led Revolution

The Problem: Distress sales. [span_0](start_span)Farmers harvest their crops only to find the market flooded, forcing them to sell at a loss or dump their produce[span_0](end_span). This is a "Cobweb Theorem" failure—production based on past prices rather than future demand.

The Intervention: We designed a demand-led intelligence platform. [span_1](start_span)By capturing real-time demand from B2B vendors before the sowing season, we provide farmers with a "pre-sowing intelligence" report[span_1](end_span).

The Behavioral Nudge

Farmers are risk-averse. To build trust, we conceptualized a "Not-for-Profit-for-Farmers" model. The platform is free for them; revenue comes from the logistics and vendor matching. [span_2](start_span)This removes the "middleman suspicion" bias[span_2](end_span).

Narrative Framing in Policy Adoption

[span_3](start_span)Hypothesis: Indian tomato farmers reject beneficial policies not because the policies are bad, but because they are communicated via dry, bureaucratic language[span_3](end_span).

Methodology: A between-subjects experimental study. We present the same policy to two groups: Group A gets the standard government circular. [span_4](start_span)Group B gets a "Narrative Frame"—a story rooted in socio-cultural context[span_4](end_span).

Cook2You: Frictionless Nutrition

The Gap: A disconnect between the desire for healthy eating and the lack of time to cook. [span_7](start_span)Existing solutions (delivery) were unhealthy; hiring chefs was untrustworthy[span_7](end_span).

The Solution: A platform connecting families with verified personal chefs. [span_8](start_span)We built trust through a rigorous verification system and "Ethical Sourcing" badges for ingredients, appealing to the user's desire for safety and quality[span_8](end_span).

FarMarGro: The Subscription Box Model

Designed an integrated marketplace for organic and indigenous produce. To ensure transparency, we conceptualized a "Farmer Story" feature attached to every product, leveraging the Identifiable Victim Effect (positive version) to build consumer empathy.

We launched a subscription box model for traditional Tamil Nadu rice varieties, ensuring consistent revenue for farmers while offering convenience to urban buyers.

"The goal is not just to analyze behavior, but to design for it."
