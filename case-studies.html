A deeper look into the interventions, the data, and the results.

KriShiKsha: The Demand-Led Revolution

The Problem: Agricultural workers frequently face distress sales. They bring their harvest to market only to realize there is a massive oversupply, leading to collapsed prices and wasted food.

The Intervention: We built a proactive intelligence network. By gathering live requirements from commercial buyers prior to the planting phase, we deliver predictive insights directly to rural communities.

Cook2You: Frictionless Nutrition

The Gap: Urban professionals want nutritious, home-cooked meals but lack the time and energy to prepare them after long workdays.

The Solution: An intuitive booking ecosystem that links households with vetted culinary professionals. We integrated ethical ingredient sourcing and stringent verification to build immediate consumer confidence.
