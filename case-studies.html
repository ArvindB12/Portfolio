Startup Initiative
Agri-Intelligence

KriShiKsha: The Demand-Led Revolution

The Problem: Agricultural workers frequently face distress sales. They bring their harvest to market only to realize there is a massive oversupply, leading to collapsed prices and wasted food. This happens because planting decisions are reactive, based on last year's trends rather than future needs.

The Intervention: We built a proactive intelligence network. By gathering live requirements from commercial buyers prior to the planting phase, we deliver predictive insights directly to rural communities.

The Behavioral Nudge

Risk-averse users need high trust. We structured this as a zero-cost model for the growers, generating revenue entirely through logistics routing and vendor-side matching. This structure actively neutralizes suspicion of exploitation.

Policy & Psychology

Narrative Framing in Policy Adoption

Hypothesis: Many growers reject helpful government initiatives not due to the initiative's flaws, but because the information is delivered in rigid, bureaucratic formats that cause cognitive friction.

Methodology: We set up a controlled experiment. One group receives standard official documentation. The second group receives the exact same information wrapped in culturally relevant storytelling.

Health Tech

Cook2You: Frictionless Nutrition

The Gap: Urban professionals want nutritious, home-cooked meals but lack the time and energy to prepare them after long workdays. Existing delivery apps focus on fast food, and hiring private staff feels inaccessible.

The Solution: An intuitive booking ecosystem that links households with vetted culinary professionals. We integrated ethical ingredient sourcing and stringent verification to build immediate consumer confidence.

FarMarGro: The Subscription Box Model

An end-to-end marketplace focusing on indigenous and chemical-free yields. To build consumer trust and empathy, we attached specific "Grower Profiles" to the inventory, utilizing the psychological impact of identifiable narratives.

We introduced a recurring delivery model for native rice grains, securing reliable income streams for rural partners while providing unmatched convenience for city residents.

"The goal is not just to analyze behavior, but to design for it."
